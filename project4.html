Tutorial: Calculus for Engineers

Subject Code: BAS-103 / KAS-103T

Target Audience: First Year B.Tech (CSIT)

Module 1: Differential Calculus

  • Leibnitz's Theorem: Formula to calculate the nth derivative of the product of two functions.
  • Taylor's & Maclaurin's Series: Approximation of functions around a specific point.
  • Partial Derivatives: Rates of change of multi-variable functions.

Module 2: Multivariable Calculus

  • Euler's Theorem: For homogeneous functions of degree n.
  • Jacobians: Coordinate transformation and substitution in multiple integrals.
  • Maxima & Minima: Finding extreme values of functions with two variables.

Module 3: Multiple Integrals

  • Double and Triple Integrals
  • Change of Order of Integration
  • Applications to Area and Volume calculations

Important Practice Questions

  1. Find the n-th derivative of y = x^2 * sin(x) using Leibnitz's Theorem.
  2. Verify Euler's Theorem for u = (x^2 + y^2) / (x + y).
  3. Evaluate the double integral of (x + y) dx dy over the region bounded by x=0, y=0, and x+y=1.
